Delete your Swipess account

You can delete your account inside the Swipess app:

  1. Open Swipess and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap DELETE ACCOUNT and confirm.

This permanently removes your profile, listings, messages, and stored files. It cannot be undone.

If you cannot open the app, email support@swipess.com from the address on the account.
